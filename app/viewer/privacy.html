Privacy Policy

Your documents, models, and computational code belong exclusively to you. Learn how LDOC Studio guarantees 100% air-gapped autonomy and zero data leakage.

Effective: September 12, 2026 • Standard Version 2.6.0 • J AI ENTERPRISES

01. Zero-Telemetry & Air-Gapped Autonomy

At J AI ENTERPRISES, our foundational architectural principle is that Living Documents (.ldocx) are sovereign files. Unlike cloud-locked document platforms (such as Figma, Notion, or Canva), LDOC Studio operates entirely client-side inside your browser or native desktop application.

The Air-Gap Guarantee: LDOC Studio executes zero cloud telemetry on your document contents, AST structures, embedded 3D models (GLB/OBJ), or live JavaScript calculations. When running disconnected or within classified SCIF environments, zero packets egress your local device.

02. Information We Collect & Process

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. The limited categories of data we process include:

  • Account Registration: If you create an account on our cloud portal, we store your email address, hashed password, and user identifier to authenticate you.
  • Commercial Transactions: Payment processing is handled securely by Stripe. We do not store raw credit card numbers or banking credentials on our servers.
  • Enterprise Inquiries: Contact information submitted via enterprise lead forms is used solely to respond to your inquiry and provide quotes.
  • Zero Payload Ingestion: Your .ldocx packages, presentation slides, code sandboxes, and cryptographic keys are NEVER uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly configure a custom webhook.

03. Local Storage & Cryptographic Keys

LDOC Studio uses standard web storage technologies (localStorage, IndexedDB, and the File System Access API) strictly on your local machine to:

  • Cache your working document drafts locally so you never lose work on reload.
  • Store your personal ECDSA P-256 public/private keypairs generated via WebCrypto for local document signing.
  • Preserve user interface settings, theme preferences, and editor keybindings.

You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings or by invoking the local reset command in the Studio settings modal.

04. Execution Sandboxing & Security Isolation

When running interactive charts, WebGL 3D scenes, or JavaScript code blocks within an .ldocx file, all scripts execute inside un-privileged, origin-isolated <iframe sandbox="allow-scripts"> containers. The code is prevented from accessing your parent cookies, session tokens, or local network resources.

05. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

In accordance with global data protection laws (GDPR EU 2016/679 and CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, rectify, or request permanent deletion of your account metadata. Because document content is stored exclusively on your device, you maintain immediate physical sovereignty over all intellectual property created with LDOC Studio.
