## What is LDOC Viewer?
In accordance with the open standard model established by John Warnock (Adobe Acrobat Reader) and outlined in the LDOC strategy playbook:
- **100% Free**: Anyone in the world can read, interact with, present, and inspect `.ldocx` living presentations with zero licensing fees or subscription barriers.
- **Native 3D Holograms**: Fluid 60 FPS WebGL rendering of CAD models, sports car chassis, Tourbillon escapements, and atomic unit cells.
- **Reactive Telemetry & Physics**: Dynamic sliders for quantum Schrödinger wavefunction superpositions, gear escapement frequency tuners, and live telemetry feeds.
- **Multi-Mode Presentation**: Switch instantly between Tactile Paper, Frosted Glass, Keynote Deck, and Living Gazette reading modes.

---

## Desktop Usage (Windows)
Run `LDOC-Viewer.exe` or open `index.html` in any modern browser.  
Drag and drop any `.ldocx` presentation directly into the window.
