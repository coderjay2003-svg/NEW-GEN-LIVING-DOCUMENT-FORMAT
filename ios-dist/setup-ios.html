Install LDOC Suite on iOS

Install the 100% Offline Living Document (.ldocx) Viewer & Editor directly to your iPhone or iPad Home Screen.

1
Tap the Share Button (⎙ / ⬆) in Safari's bottom toolbar.
2
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (⊞).
3
Tap "Add" at the top right. The app will launch in fullscreen with full offline 3D capabilities!
